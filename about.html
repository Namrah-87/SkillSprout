About SkillSprout

SkillSprout is a GT project created by Mohid Atif with the purpose to help kids and adults learn about everything tech. We offer online courses on many topics each with it's own quizzes and tests (yes, you do have to score a 65 or more to move on...).

What makes SkillSprout different?

SkillSprout is unique because everything is completely and 101% free, though AI use is limited on a daily reset schedule. You also learn by DOING and not reading endless pages about the topic. Want to learn how to code? Then in SkillSprout, you will be given a project to work on, a guide book with all the important stuff, and instructions on what to do.

How do the courses work?

The courses are structured with quizzes and tests between lessons. Like mentioned earlier, in SkillSprout you learn by DOING not just by reading about the topic.
